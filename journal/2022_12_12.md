1. The problem I am addressing is how to learn from data coming from different environments.

Heterogeneity between environments is most often encountered when talking about failure cases for existing tasks, like predicting in the presence of a confounder.

However, we are also showing how harnessing heterogeneity allows us to perform new functions.

Through this work, I claim to expand mixed-effects models rather than replace them. All the models are technically mixed-effects models, but the algorithms for fitting and inference in mixed-models are too slow for dealing with high-dimensional data. I am using the mechanics of deep learning and amortised variational inference in order to fit the parameters and infer the variables of mixed-effects models in situations with high-dimensional image, climate or audio data.
