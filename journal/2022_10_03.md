- **Floating ideas.**
  - **Generative modifications network.** True learning happens by modifying the ground-truth in such a way that it remains in the correct distribution. This is different from the common way to train generative models, where you either try to match the ground-truth reconstruction (VAE), or you try to fool a ground-truth evaluator (GAN), or measure the conditional probability (PixelRNN), or match the ground-truth to a high likelihood point in the latent space (Flows), or invert a corruption process (diffusion).
  - **Generativee wild shot network.** Sample the prior distribution of a generator and optimise loss only on the closest sample.
