## Collaborative filtering with Group-Instance Disentanglement

- [Rendle2020Neural](https://api.semanticscholar.org/CorpusID:218719424) show that, for collaborative filtering models based on user and item embeddings, it is better to use a dot-product as the operator combining the embeddings, instead of using an MLP. They show, for implicit feedback (only 0s and 1s), that the MLP is only better than the dot product when there is a lot of data and the embeddings are quite small.

- There seem to be few works which use an encoder to infer user/item embeddings from the ratings.
