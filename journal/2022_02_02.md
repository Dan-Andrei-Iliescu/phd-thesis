## Preparation

Who is my audience? People from the disentanglement literature. I want them to change their methodology to reflect how disentanglement can be applied to other problems. It would also be interesting for people dealing with those other problems.

I want to use group-instance disentanglement to perform multiple imputation on a clinical dataset with grouped data. Multiple imputations are achieved by translating observations from multiple patients into this patient. This is different from re-sampling the dataset and re-training the model.

Causal inference of treatment effects.

Group instance disentanglement turns categorical variables into continuous, making it easier to find similarity between groups or generalise to new groups.

Repeated measurements, time series cross-sectional data.

### Contribution

I want to propose to the representation learning community a new application area for disentanglement, namely the tasks that have previously been solved by multi-level models.

### Why do people use multi-level models rather than single-level?

|                      | No pooling                                                                                                  | Pooling                                                                                | Multi-level                                                                                                              |
| -------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Data Prediction      | Do per-group prediction. Use fraction of the data. Cannot extrapolate to new groups.                        | Use all of the data. | Parameter Estimation | Give widely varying estimates for the treatment effect. Could infer a measure of similarity between groups. | Same estimate of the treatment effect for every group. Gives no measure of similarity. | Gives different confidence intervals for the treatment effect across groups. Gives measure of similarity between groups. |

### What can group-instance disentanglement do better?

- **Data imputation:** More expressive generative model. Novel re-sampling through translation. When different covariates are missing some of the time, the model can deal with them all at once.
- **Parameter estimation:** Group-instance could do the same things, but quicker because of amortised inference.
