## Causal de Finetti: On the Identification of Invariant Causal Structure in Exchangeable Data

[Guo2022Causal](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2203.15756.pdf). Causal inference promises to make statistics able to answer questions about interventions/counterfactuals. Simple factorisation of the conditional distribution can only identify the causal graph up to a broad class of equivalence. Another approach is to observe the same causal graph under different environments.

The principle of *invariant causal mechansims* states that the conditional distributions which form the causal graph do not change from one environment to another, and change independently of one another.
