## Discussion with Omer

- What is the point of a hierarchical model?
- Can any Gaussian process be copied by a single latent-variable model?

Gaussian process: $p(f^*, f^{**} | \bar{f}) \neq p(f^* | \bar{f}) ~ p(f^{**} | \bar{f})$

Neural process: $p(f^*, f^{**} | \bar{f}) = \int_z p(f^* | z, \bar{f}) ~ p(f^{**} | z, \bar{f}) p(z | \bar{f}) dz$
