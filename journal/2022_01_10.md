## Goal of disentanglement

> *The goal of disentangled representations is to learn a function $r(x)$ mapping each observation to a low-dimensional latent code, such that each dimension corresponds to exactly one factor of variation, contains all the information from that factor, and none of the information from the other factors.*

1. The goal is something that everyone agrees with, to train a representation network with certain properties.
2. The state-of-the-art approach is to train a generative model via variational inference with certain additional constraints.

In my model of disentanglement (ground-truth and inference) I don't need indices for the random variables since this is a model of a repeating process, not a dataset.
