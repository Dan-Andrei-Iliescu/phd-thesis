# How to write a good paper

[GDrive](https://drive.google.com/file/d/1sE4ZCHkU65J6ZFjOK4dWGIiuPygkM6ZW/view)

- Scientific community is like a crowded marketplace. You must start by saying
  - what the paper is about
  - what problem it solves
  - why the problem is interesting
  - what is new and what isn't
  - why it's so neat
- Show simple toy example to let people understand the main idea.

## How to structure a paper
1. Start by stating which problem you are addressing. They must care about it, which means that you might need to tell them why they care.
2. State what other solutions there are to this problem, and why they aren't satisfactory. If they were satisfactory, you wouldn't need to do the work.
3. Explain your solutions, and show why and how it is better.
4. Talk about related work, where similar solutions and methodologies have been applied to different problems.

## How to end a paper
- Conclusions, summary of the paper, results
- What this opens up, how it changes how we approach problems
- No future work, you don't get partial credit for things you wanted to do but didn't

## Writing tips
- Keep your reader upmost in your mind. What do they know so far? What do they expect next? Treat them like a guest in your house, anticipate their needs.
- Write from a position of security not competition. Compliment other work, recommend that the reader visits other work.
- Figures should be self-contained. They should state in the caption what the reader should notice.
- Sentences should flow smoothly even when equations are replaced by "blah".
- Identify each equation by a phrase, not only by a number.
