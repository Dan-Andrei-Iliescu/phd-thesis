\textit{Dear members of the panel,}

I am writing to express my keen interest in the 2023 Schmidt Science Fellowship Programme. This fellowship represents a unique opportunity for me to pivot from computer science to psychology and make a significant impact on the science of wellbeing. With a strong background in Machine Learning, a dedicated approach to interdisciplinary research, and a firm commitment to advancing social good, I am well-aligned with the objectives of the programme.

As a Schmidt Science Fellow, I plan to investigate Psychological Buffering within the framework of positive psychology. Psychological Buffering refers to the protective effects of nurturing psychologically healthy behaviors, such as warm relationships, gratitude, and optimism, which can mitigate trauma and enhance lifelong wellbeing. For instance, research suggests that responding to stress with altruism can help prevent depression later in life~\autocite{vaillant_ego_1994}.

Understanding Psychological Buffering is of immense societal importance. This concept may hold the key to explaining why some individuals emerge stronger from adverse life events, while others suffer lasting harm~\autocite{seligman_positive_2000}. Moreover, uncovering the relationship between mental behaviors and lifelong wellbeing has the potential to revolutionise personalized medicine. By identifying the causal mechanisms underpinning individual-level predictions, we can develop applications to monitor user behaviors and provide risk assessments, empowering people to make informed decisions about their lives~\autocite{bonderud_how_2021}.

Achieving this goal requires a pioneering, interdisciplinary approach that transcends the boundaries of a single scientific discipline. Psychological Buffering lies at the intersection of psychology, public health, and epidemiology, all of which have mature statistical methods for population-level inference. However, they are ill-equipped to make causal predictions at the individual level. Complications include high-dimensional unstructured data, non-linear relationships between variables, and outcomes that take a lifetime to manifest~\autocite{sharma_clinical_2021}.

My scientific background uniquely positions me to bridge these gaps in the study of Psychological Buffering. My PhD research has centered on developing cutting-edge Machine Learning techniques to address confounding in high-dimensional data, which can enable AI models to generate accurate causal representations of the world. The integration of foundational AI models with traditional statistical methods used in epidemiology and psychology holds is very promising~\autocite{lauritsen_framing_2021}.

I fully embrace interdisciplinary research. I have successfully connected my work to various application areas such as Facial Recognition, Speech Synthesis, Climate Science, and Data Visualization through diverse collaborations including an internship at a London-based startup, a research consultancy for a Paris-based startup, and a partnership with the Alan Turing Institute, the UK's leading data science research facility.

My research is characterized by curiosity and creativity, driven by continuous innovation and the adoption of new technologies such as representation learning and causal inference. I have published my work at top-tier conferences and showcased my unique blend of Machine Learning expertise and artistic pursuits through an AI-generated award-winning triptych displayed at St. Edmund's College, Cambridge.

Throughout my academic journey, I have demonstrated leadership and a commitment to social good. I have engaged with the broader research community by leading projects, supervising undergraduate students, organizing the Machine Learning reading group at the Computer Laboratory, and presenting my research to diverse audiences. I am devoted to using my skills and knowledge to positively impact society, as evidenced by my involvement in social initiatives in Romania, including a website assisting Ukrainian refugees~\autocite{dopomoha} and an AI ChatBot connecting citizens with their parliamentary representatives~\autocite{ion}.

I have a clear vision for how the Schmidt Fellowship will facilitate my research goals. I aim to join the Positive Psychology Center at the University of Pennsylvania, a world leader in the study of wellbeing. This placement will enable me to deepen my understanding of Psychological Buffering and collaborate with renowned researchers in the field. My research plan involves identifying key mental behaviors to assess, collecting relevant observational data, and developing innovative techniques for personalized medicine using state-of-the-art machine learning.

In conclusion, I am confident that my achievements, intellectual curiosity, and strong alignment with the programme ethos make me an great candidate for the Schmidt Science Fellowship. I am eager to embark on this research journey and look forward to the possibility of joining the distinguished community of Schmidt Science Fellows.

\textit{Yours faithfully,}
